Why this exists

Phones can't sign in to the Windows domain, so the automatic name-matching never sees them — their activity shows as unattributed. Mapping a device's reserved IP to a person here names it in the reports. It only works if the device has a reserved (static) IP on the router, otherwise the address changes and the mapping drifts to the wrong person.
